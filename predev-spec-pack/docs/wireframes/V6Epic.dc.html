BILL-142 · EPIC · PRFAQ NOT YET ALIGNED Owner Priya · Decider Head of Product

API customers now pay for what they use, and can see it as they go

Usage pricing replaces the flat monthly fee, with fair charges when you change plan and no surprise jumps for existing customers.

The problem

Every customer pays the same flat fee today. Heavy users underpay, light users leave, and 9 of 40 surveyed customers say they were overcharged after upgrading. Survey · kickoff

What changes

Customers are billed monthly for the calls they make. Plan changes are charged by the day. Existing flat-fee accounts are capped at today's fee while they move over.

“We budget by department. We'd only switch if we can see it in real time.” Acme, discovery call 24 Sep, 14:32 · a real quote, not written by us · it's the bar we have to clear

How we'll know it worked

Billing disputes halve within two billing cycles, with no rise in churn among flat-fee accounts. Priya · baseline 47 disputes per 90 days
